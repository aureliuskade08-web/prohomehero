Cabinet styles and storage

  • Shaker cabinets

    The classic five-piece door with a simple frame. It suits traditional and modern kitchens alike and shows up throughout our recent work.

  • Pantry towers

    Full-height cabinets give you storage for dry goods and small appliances without taking up counter space.

  • Glass-front uppers

    Glass doors let you show off dishes and break up a long wall of closed cabinets.

  • Two-tone kitchens

    Lighter uppers with darker base cabinets, such as white over navy or sage, add depth without making the room feel heavy.

Choices that change the look

  • Color

    White and cream keep a kitchen bright. Sage, green and navy add character. Our finishes designer lets you preview colors in a kitchen before you decide.

  • Hardware

    Brass, matte black and polished nickel pulls change the feel of the same cabinet. Long bar pulls read modern, smaller knobs read traditional.

  • Storage

    Think about what you reach for every day. Deep drawers for pots, a pantry tower, or a stacked glass upper can matter more than the door style.
