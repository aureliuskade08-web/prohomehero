What a full remodel covers

  • Layout and design

    We measure the room, talk through how you cook and entertain, and settle the layout before anything is ordered.

  • Demolition and preparation

    The old kitchen comes out and the room is made ready for the new cabinets, counters and finishes.

  • Cabinets, counters and backsplash

    Shaker or flat-panel cabinetry, quartz or granite counters, and tile or slab backsplash chosen to work together.

  • Sinks, lighting and flooring

    Sinks and faucets, pendant and under-cabinet lighting, and plank or tile flooring finish the room.

Questions to settle early

  • Keep the layout or change it

    Keeping the sink and range where they are is simpler. Moving them or opening a wall to the next room changes the plan, and we talk through which is worth it for your kitchen.

  • Where to spend and where to save

    Cabinets and counters are usually the biggest line items. We help you decide where a higher-end finish matters to you and where a simpler one does the job.

  • Choosing finishes together

    Colors and materials look different in a real kitchen than on a sample. Our finishes designer on the home page lets you try combinations before you commit.
