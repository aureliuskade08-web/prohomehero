A kitchen island sized to your room

An island adds space, storage and seating when it fits. We size it to your room.

Get a free estimate Call (470) 573-5890

What an island can do

  • Seating

    Stools along one side turn the island into a place for breakfast, homework and company while you cook.

  • Storage

    Drawers and cabinets on the working side keep pots, baking sheets and small appliances close.

  • Statement finishes

    Paint the island a different color from the perimeter cabinets, or run the counter down the sides in a waterfall edge.

  • Peninsula option

    When the room is too narrow for an island, a peninsula still adds seating and counter space.

Sizing it right

  • Room to move

    A common guideline is to leave about three to four feet of walkway around an island. We measure your kitchen to see what fits.

  • Seating overhang

    Stools need legroom, usually about a foot of overhang. We plan that into the counter so it is comfortable and supported.

  • Lighting above

    Pendant lights over the island make it a focal point. See the lighting and flooring page for ideas.

  • Will an island fit in my kitchen?

    We measure at the free estimate and tell you honestly if an island, a smaller one or a peninsula suits the room better.

  • Can I preview island colors?

    Yes. In the finishes designer, choose an island color separate from your cabinets.
