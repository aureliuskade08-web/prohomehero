Sinks and fixtures you will use every day

The sink is where most kitchen work happens. We help you choose a sink, faucet and hardware.

Get a free estimate Call (470) 573-5890

Options we install

  • Farmhouse sinks

    A deep apron-front sink is a classic look and handles large pots and pans.

  • Workstation sinks

    Built-in ledges hold cutting boards and colanders so the sink doubles as prep space.

  • Pull-down faucets

    A spout that pulls down makes rinsing the sink and filling pots easier. Brass and matte black finishes are both popular in recent kitchens.

  • Pot fillers

    A faucet mounted over the range lets you fill a pot where you cook, so you carry less water.

Finishing touches

  • Match the metals

    Choose brass, matte black or polished nickel for the faucet, then repeat it in cabinet pulls and lighting. Try combinations in the finishes designer.

  • Mounting style

    An undermount sink sits below the counter for a smooth edge. A farmhouse sink shows its front panel. The counter material and cabinet base decide which works.

  • Think about use

    If you cook a lot, a larger single bowl or a workstation sink may beat a double bowl. We help you choose.

    Fixtures are one of our services. Describe what you have in mind in the estimate form and we will tell you what is involved.
